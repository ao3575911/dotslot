
### i1 '_' padding in Markdown: two bare slots on a line render as emphasis (markdown-it-py, CommonMark)
'Fill <./_____> and <./_____> here'
   -> <p>Fill &lt;./<em><strong><strong>&gt; and &lt;./</strong></strong></em>&gt; here</p>
'A <./a:_x_> and <./b:_y_>'
   -> <p>A &lt;./a:<em>x</em>&gt; and &lt;./b:<em>y</em>&gt;</p>
'Owner: <./owner:Adam________>  Date: <./__________>'
   -> <p>Owner: &lt;./owner:Adam________&gt;  Date: &lt;./__________&gt;</p>
'Fill `<./_____>` and `<./_____>` here'
   -> <p>Fill <code>&lt;./_____&gt;</code> and <code>&lt;./_____&gt;</code> here</p>
